📺 How To Calibrate With Left Side OSD

When you press "Settings / Picture" on your TV remote, your TV menu overlays precisely over this left panel. Review the target checklist below, open your TV menu, then observe the test patterns on the right half in real time!

0. Picture Mode Preset & Backlight Base Settings
  • Select Cinema, Movie, or Filmmaker Mode (turn off "Vivid" or "Dynamic").
  • Set Color Temperature to Warm2 / Warm (standard D65 6500K).
  • Adjust Backlight / OLED Light according to room ambient light. Do not confuse with "Brightness".
1. Brightness (Black Level) Pattern [1]

Controls shadow detail and the black floor (RGB 16 in video broadcast space).

• Bar 16 (Reference Black) must melt into pure black background.
• Bar 17 and above should be faintly distinguishable.
• If 17 is missing = crushed blacks (raise brightness). If background looks foggy gray = washed out (lower brightness).
2. Contrast (White Level) Pattern [2]

Sets highlight clipping and maximum luminance without tint shift or eye strain.

• Steps up to 234 & 235 (Nominal White) must remain visible.
• 236–254 (WTW / Whiter-Than-White) may faintly flash without turning pink or cyan.
• If all high bars clip into a solid flat white blob, lower Contrast.
3. Sharpness & Edge Enhancer Pattern [4]

TV Sharpness filters create artificial artificial edges and jagged artifacts.

• Examine the 1-pixel alternating line grids and crosshair targets.
• Rule of thumb: Lower Sharpness until glowing white halos/fringes vanish.
• On most modern TVs, true neutral sharpness is 0% (Sony/LG) or 10% (Samsung).
4. Color & Saturation Pattern [3]

Ensure color gradients are continuous without banding or oversaturation blowouts.

• Colors should look vivid yet natural, especially skin tones.
• Red and magenta gradient swatches must not lose gradations in the top 20%.
